    How to use it

    • Study: turn it to Study and the countdown starts. Tap to pause, double-tap to restart, hold to end the session.
    • Break: turn back to Home after a session for a break. Leave early (after a minute) for a half break; under a minute it just pauses.
    • Tasks: stand it up for the list and QR code. Tap a task to check it, swipe the list to scroll.
    • Clear: stand it upside down to slash the checked tasks away.
    • Settings: hold BOOT (or the B key) for over a second. Tap − / + to change study and break length. Swipe left on the screen, or press BOOT briefly, for brightness and direction; swipe right to come back.
    • Carry: knock ●● ● to lock it for the bag, again to unlock.

    Your tasks and settings are kept in this browser. Leave it untouched and it dims, then sleeps, just like the device.
